# Task: FxSound V0.0.4 - True Mastering-Grade Spectral Mid-Side Engine

## Root Causes of Failure in V0.0.3
1. **Legacy FxSound Surround Phase Inversion**: `dfxDsp.processAudio` was altering phase angles in an uncontrolled vintage 2003 PC algorithm, forcing negative correlation (-0.45).
2. **Nonlinear Waveshaping Distortion**: `applySolidSaturation` exploded with polynomial multiplication on high inputs, generating harsh clipping when Transient Punch was increased.
3. **Single High Band Instead of True Spectral Tri-Band**: Everything above 140 Hz was treated as a single monolith, smearing vocal mids and high air together.

## Engineering Architecture for V0.0.4
1. **Tri-Band Linkwitz-Riley LR4 Crossover (4th order, 24 dB/oct)**:
   - Low Band (< 140 Hz): 100% Mono anchor.
   - Mid Band (140 Hz - 3500 Hz): Core vocal & instrument body.
   - High Band (> 3500 Hz): Air & spatial diffusion.
2. **Crest-Factor Dynamic Transient Punch (Ozone Impact Architecture)**:
   - Sample-by-sample Crest Factor detector in Mid Band: $\text{CF} = \text{Peak}_{fast} / (\text{RMS}_{slow} + \epsilon)$.
   - Dynamic transient gain applied cleanly to Mid channel (0 to +3 dB) with analog tanh ceiling protection. Zero harsh distortion.
3. **Spectral Mid-Side Energy Limiter (Mathematical Phase Guarantee)**:
   - In both Mid and High bands, Side energy is constrained by $E_S \le 0.40 \cdot E_M$.
   - Phase correlation $\rho = (E_M - E_S) / (E_M + E_S) \ge +0.43$ is mathematically guaranteed across 100% of knob movements.
4. **Pristine Spatial Expansion in High Band**:
   - Stereo expansion focused on the Air band (> 3.5 kHz) where spatial depth blooms naturally without hollowing out lead vocals.
5. **Headless Perceptual Loudness Lock (Auto-Gain)**:
   - Output RMS is continuously locked to Input RMS with smooth ballistics, ensuring 0 dB volume difference between active and bypass.
