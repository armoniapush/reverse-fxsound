# Task: FxSound V0.0.3 - Spectral Transient Engine & Auto-Gain (Ozone 12 & FabFilter Pro-MB Architecture)

## Context & Objectives
- **Problem diagnosed in V0.0.2**:
  1. Volume jump (+3 to +5 dB) on bypass/active causing psychoacoustic loudness bias.
  2. Loss of correlation (-0.32 to -0.50 in video) at high macro values due to static side amplification.
  3. Indiscriminate widening blurring the drum/vocal transients into the stereo field.
- **Solution (Reverse-engineered from Ozone 12 & Pro-MB)**:
  1. **Dual-Ballistic Envelope Follower (Fast/Slow)**: Separates transients from sustain without heavy CPU FFTs.
  2. **Mid-Side Transient Separation**: Anatomic punch locked to Mid channel on transients; spatial bloom applied to Side channel on sustain.
  3. **Headless Adaptive Phase Guard**: Automatic dynamic side ducking if correlation drops below +0.35, physically guaranteeing mono-safety.
  4. **Headless Perceptual Auto-Gain (RMS matching)**: Output is continuously matched to dry input loudness, removing the volume jump on bypass.
